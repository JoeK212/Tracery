TRACERY is a browser tool for exploring the geometry of architectural windows, from Romanesque and Gothic to Islamic, Art Nouveau and modern. It draws the compass construction in 2D beside a 3D model with separate stone, tracery and glass layers.

Choose from twelve heads, including pointed, four-centred, ogee and horseshoe, and six patterns: mullions, geometric tracery, rose windows, grids (golden ratio, Fibonacci or Modulor), Mondrian compositions and Islamic lattices. Shuffle makes quick variations, and a section cut shows the wall in profile. Lengths show in millimetres or feet-inches, and the window exports as SVG or DXF, flat or as a 3D mesh.

The Help button explains the math behind every result at three levels, from student to professional.

It is a single index.html with no build step. Open it in a browser or deploy the folder to Netlify. The tool needs a tablet or desktop screen, but the Help guide also opens on a phone.
